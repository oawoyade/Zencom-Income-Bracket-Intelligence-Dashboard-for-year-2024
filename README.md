# 💼 Zencom Income Bracket Intelligence Dashboard (2024)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an analytical breakdown of income distribution across demographics, education levels, occupations, and regions within the Zencom ecosystem for the year 2024. The primary objective is to identify patterns in earning potential, assess the impact of lifestyle and geographic factors, and uncover opportunities for strategic workforce planning, compensation reviews, and regional investments.

---

## 🗂️ Data Source

The dataset used in this analysis is a synthetic income report generated for Zencom’s internal workforce intelligence program. It includes data points such as gender, region, commute distance, education level, homeownership, occupation, age group, and total income.

---

## 🛠️ Tools and Methodology

- **Excel**: Used for data modeling, DAX calculations, visualizations, and interactive dashboard creation.
- **Excel**: Used for initial data cleaning and exploration.
- **Data Segmentation**: The data was segmented by income bracket (Executive, Mid-Level, Entry-Level, and Others), age, region, education, and gender for multi-dimensional analysis.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 📈 Demographic Trends
- Males earn approximately **$3,000 more** on average than females, revealing a gender pay gap.
- **Middle-aged professionals** represent the most financially productive demographic.

### 🎓 Education and Occupation
- A **clear correlation** exists between education level and earning potential.
- **Professional** and **management roles** consistently yield the highest income.
- Educational fields such as **Life Sciences** and **Medical** have high-income potential, yet may face higher attrition or dissatisfaction.

### 🌎 Regional Analysis
- **North America** drives more than 50% of total income, suggesting strong regional economic influence and performance.
- Other global regions underperform comparatively, highlighting potential for growth or policy intervention.

### 🏠 Lifestyle & Commute Behavior
- Shorter commutes (or remote work) are tied to higher income brackets.
- Homeownership is a strong predictor of financial stability and higher income.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Upskill Entry-Level Employees**
   - Provide training programs and scholarship support to elevate education levels and earning potential.

2. **Close the Gender Pay Gap**
   - Conduct salary audits and introduce equitable compensation frameworks to ensure fairness.

3. **Invest in Remote Work Infrastructure**
   - Given the strong performance among short-commute earners, Zencom should continue to support hybrid or remote models to attract and retain talent.

4. **Capitalize on Professional Talent**
   - Retain and develop professionals by offering growth paths, bonuses, and mentorship to maintain high-income contributions.

5. **Expand Regional Investment in Europe & Pacific**
   - Bridge income disparity through localized development initiatives, new role placements, and skill-building campaigns.
